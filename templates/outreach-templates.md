# Outreach and reply templates

Short, honest, personal. Fill every `<…>`; delete what doesn't apply. One follow-up at most, 5–7 days later. Never send in bulk, never promise anything in exchange for a link, never misrepresent who you are. The user reviews and sends; the AI only drafts. Rules: references/40-links-and-mentions.md §B4 and core rules §8.

## 1. Unlinked brand mention

Subject: Thanks for mentioning <Brand> in "<article title>"

> Hi <Name>,
>
> I'm <your name>, <role> at <Brand>. Thanks for including us in "<article title>". <One specific, genuine sentence about the piece.>
>
> If it's useful to your readers, would you consider linking "<Brand>" to <specific URL>? It covers <what the reader gets>. No worries at all if not.
>
> <Name>, <Brand>

## 2. List or resource inclusion ("best of" list, resource page, awesome list)

Subject: Suggestion for "<list title>"

> Hi <Name>,
>
> I work at <Brand> (so I'm biased, to be upfront). Your list "<list title>" covers <category>. We make <one-line factual description>, and it may fit for readers who need <specific use case>: <key differentiator with evidence, e.g. "free plan up to 3 users", "ships to India">.
>
> Details: <URL>. Happy to send a trial or answer questions. If it isn't a fit, thanks anyway for keeping the list useful.
>
> <Name>, <role>, <Brand>

For a GitHub awesome list, use the list's format in the PR: `- [<Brand>](<URL>) - <factual one-line description>.` and add "Disclosure: I work at <Brand>." to the PR body.

## 3. Podcast or guest pitch

>

> 2. <topic> — <one-line takeaway>
> 3. <topic> — <one-line takeaway>
>

>
> <Name>

## 4. Data story pitch to a journalist

Subject: New data: <one-line finding>

> Hi <Name>,
>
> You covered <their related story>. We just published <study name>: <finding in one sentence with the number>. Method: <sample size, dates, source>, full methodology and data at <URL>.
>
> <Expert name, title> can comment. Charts are free to use with a credit link.
>
> <Name>, <Brand> (I work for <Brand>)

## Community reply disclosure lines

Use one whenever the reply mentions the brand, a client or an employer:

- "Disclosure: I work at <Brand>."
- "Full disclosure: I'm the founder of <Brand>, so weigh this accordingly."
- "I work with <Brand> (client of mine), so I'm biased; here are other options too: <A>, <B>."

Reply shape: answer the question first (steps, numbers, trade-offs) → mention the brand as one option only if it truly fits → link the specific page or none → disclosure line.
